Ultima V Redux / Differences you'll notice

Differences you'll notice

Each change, the original next to Redux. Switchable ones say where to turn the original rule back on.

COMBAT SWITCHABLE COMBAT-0009

Slimes and Gargoyles split once

OriginalA surviving Slime or Gargoyle can divide again and again, and so can its children.
ReduxEach splits once at most, and its child cannot split.
Switch in Configure: Repeated Monster Division
COMBAT ALWAYS ON COMBAT-0004 Original: Incomplete

Missed blows stay in range

OriginalA miss lands on a random square next to the target, even one the weapon could not reach, and can hit whoever stands there.
ReduxA miss only lands on squares within the weapon's normal range, so where you aim is where it can hurt.
PARTY ALWAYS ON PROGRESS-0003

A stat award for every level

OriginalA visit from the Wanderer gives one +1 to STR, DEX or INT, even if you gained several levels at once.
ReduxYou get one award for each level gained, and none if a level is corrected downward.
CAMPING ALWAYS ON CAMP-0001

Camp on horseback or a carpet

OriginalThe party must be on foot to camp.
ReduxHorses stay mounted. A magic carpet goes into your pack while you rest.
SHIPS ALWAYS ON SHIP-0001

Sails come down when a fight starts

OriginalSail state and sailing direction carry on after the fight.
ReduxEvery fight furls the sails, so you choose when to sail on. Your heading is kept.
DUNGEONS HELPER DNG-0004

The party remembers chests

OriginalEach Search rolls again, and a failed search invents how bad a trap is.
ReduxWhat you learn about a chest lasts the visit, and Jimmy will not waste a key on one you know is safe.
NAVIGATION HELPER MOON-0008

Located moonstones are marked for you

OriginalThe Sextant reports where you stand.
ReduxLocating a moonstone also marks it for navigation, at no extra cost or time.
Works with Flexible Sextant Use.
MOONGATES LOOK AND FEEL MOON-0003

Gates you cannot see change quietly

OriginalEvery gate rises and sinks silently.
ReduxA gate in view animates with a message. One out of sight changes at once and never stops you.
TOWNS FIXES A BUG KARMA-0004 Original: Incomplete

Cannon shots stop at walls

OriginalThe inspected cannon code can remove a target behind a wall, and its Shadowlord exclusion has no effect.
ReduxShots stop at blocking terrain, and cannons cannot remove Shadowlords.
NAVIGATION SWITCHABLE ASTRO-0006

Sextant, day or night

OriginalThe Sextant works on the world map, at night.
ReduxDay or night, on the world map and inside surface towns. A Sextant is still required, and dungeons and the Underworld are still excluded.
Switch in Configure: Flexible Sextant Use
DUNGEONS SWITCHABLE DNG-0009 Original: Incomplete

Unlit dungeons show dim outlines

OriginalWith no light, the dungeon view is black.
ReduxDim geometry shows, but you gain no light.
Switch in Configure: Show Subdued Unlit Dungeons
