Ultima V Redux

How to start playing, and every place Redux plays differently from the 1988 original. Free, non-commercial, and in public preview.

Get started

Unsigned preview builds. Platform status says what has been checked on each system.

  1. Download Get the archive for macOS, Windows or Linux from the releases page. Check it against SHA256SUMS and extract all of it. Releases page
  2. Bring the original You need the complete MS-DOS PC game folder, such as the GOG edition. The Amiga, Atari ST, Commodore 64 and Apple II versions use different files. Which files?
  3. Run it Start ultimav. First-run setup looks for your game folder, or tells you where to copy it. If setup cannot find it
  4. Bring a save (optional) The title menu imports SAVED.GAM and SAVED.OOL from your game folder. A converter handles saves kept elsewhere. Save converter

Differences you'll notice

Slimes and Gargoyles split once

OriginalA surviving Slime or Gargoyle can divide again and again, and so can its children.
ReduxEach splits once at most, and its child cannot split.

Unlit dungeons show dim outlines

OriginalWith no light, the dungeon view is black.
ReduxDim geometry shows, but you gain no light.

Sextant, day or night

OriginalThe Sextant works on the world map, at night.
ReduxDay or night, on the world map and inside surface towns. A Sextant is still required, and dungeons and the Underworld are still excluded.

Known gaps

Where Redux is not faithful yet. These are bugs to fix, not choices.

  • How monsters choose targets, flee and use special attacks. COMBAT-0008
  • Which shrine a moongate sends you to at midnight. MOON-0005
  • Where summoned creatures, Blink and Clone land when there is little room. SPELL-0005
  • Who a failed reagent mixture hurts when no one is eligible. MIX-0003
  • Where the ending text breaks its lines: Redux never makes the original's hyphenated breaks. TEXT-0003

1 more affects only rare cases and stay in the developer notes.
