Options

Gameplay settings in Configure on the title menu. Some bring back the original rule.

  • Repeated Monster Division CAN RESTORE ORIGINAL

    Original: surviving Slimes and Gargoyles can divide repeatedly. Off keeps Redux's one-child split limit; On restores repeated division.

  • Remember Hidden Dungeon Discoveries HELPER

    Remember discovered hidden doors and pits between visits. On keeps discoveries. Off resets them each fresh visit.

  • Show Subdued Unlit Dungeons CAN RESTORE ORIGINAL

    Original: an unlit dungeon viewport is black. On shows subdued geometry without granting light; Off restores binary darkness.

  • Batch Merchant Theft CAN RESTORE ORIGINAL

    On: one Faulinei theft per paid batch. Off: original theft per item, bundle or patient; theft can stop a batch early.

  • Smooth Surface Movement LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    Scroll while walking, riding, flying or boating. Mounts animate 25% faster. Gameplay timing, combat and dungeons are unchanged.

  • Concealed Turn Slots LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    On reserves blank NEXT slots for concealed enemies. Off compacts the four-action forecast to avoid revealing hidden actors. Scheduling is unchanged.

  • Flexible Sextant Use CAN RESTORE ORIGINAL

    Day or night, including surface settlement interiors. A Sextant is required; no dungeons or Underworld. Off: night use on the world map.

  • Ankh Visit Bonus CAN RESTORE ORIGINAL

    A folk tale says an Ankh draws Lord British to your camp. There's no evidence the original did this, but it's here if you enjoy the idea. On: each living party member wearing an Ankh adds 5% to the 25% visit chance. Off: the original 25%.

  • Combat Speed LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    Change how quickly combat actions and animations play. 1x is normal speed; higher values make combat faster.

  • Confirm Quick Save HELPER

    Ask before saving with Q. On asks first; Off saves immediately. Q saves to the last slot you loaded or saved. Default: On.

  • Show Combat HP Bars LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    Show health bars during combat. Off hides the bars and adds injury descriptions to the battle report. Default: On.

  • Maximum Action Popups LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    Choose how many action messages can appear over the map at once. Off hides them; messages still appear in the lower-right text area.

Wording follows the in-game descriptions. Configure also has Audio and Display sections.
