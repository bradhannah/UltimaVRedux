Ultima V Redux / Options

Options

Gameplay settings in Configure on the title menu. Some bring back the original rule.

  • Repeated Monster Division CAN RESTORE ORIGINAL

    On: a Slime or Gargoyle that survives can divide again and again, as in the original. Off: each divides once, and its child never does.

  • Remember Hidden Dungeon Discoveries HELPER

    On: hidden doors and pits you find stay found. Off: they are hidden again on your next visit.

  • Show Subdued Unlit Dungeons CAN RESTORE ORIGINAL

    On: with no light you still make out dim walls and passages, but nothing is lit. Off: no light, no view, as in the original.

  • Batch Merchant Theft CAN RESTORE ORIGINAL

    On: when gold goes missing in a shop, it happens once for the whole purchase. Off: as in the original, once for each item, bundle or patient, which can cut a purchase short.

  • Smooth Surface Movement LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    On: the map glides as you walk, ride, fly or sail. Only the look changes; play, battles and dungeons run as before.

  • Hidden Enemy Turns LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    On: an enemy you cannot see (invisible, under an arch or in the dark) still takes its place in Turn Order, as ??? unseen. Off: it is left out. The turns themselves are the same either way.

  • Flexible Sextant Use CAN RESTORE ORIGINAL

    On: the sextant works by day or night, on the world map and inside surface towns. Off: only at night on the world map, as in the original. It never works in dungeons or the Underworld.

  • Ankh Visit Bonus CAN RESTORE ORIGINAL

    An old players' tale says an Ankh draws Lord British to your camp; the original shows no sign of it. On: each living member wearing an Ankh makes his visit a little more likely. Off: the original odds.

  • Deed Experience CAN RESTORE ORIGINAL

    Pay the whole party experience for quest deeds, once each. The original pays for kills only. None: no deed experience. Lean: a steady reward along the quest. Fuller: double Lean. Deeds already done are paid when this is turned on. Experience already paid is never topped up or taken back, so the game asks before applying a change. Default: Lean.

  • Share Kill Experience HELPER

    On: every living member shares a kill's experience equally. Off: it all goes to whoever struck the killing blow, as in the original. Default: Off.

  • Combat Speed LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    Change how quickly combat actions and animations play. 1x is normal speed; higher values make combat faster.

  • Combat Key Repeat HELPER

    Hold a key in combat to repeat it, for every party member in turn. Measured repeats arrows and Tab about 6 times a second; Fast also repeats A, up to 10, 20 or 30 times a second. Default: Measured.

  • Combat Panels LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    Choose what is drawn left of the battlefield. Order + Report shows Turn Order above the Battle Report. Turn Order only and Off send battle messages to the message window. Default: Order + Report.

  • Confirm Quick Save HELPER

    On: Q asks before it saves, and Ctrl+Q before it loads. Off: Q saves and Ctrl+Q loads at once. Either way they use the slot you last saved or loaded. Default: On.

  • Show Combat HP Bars LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    Show a health bar under each injured figure in combat. Off hides the bars and adds injury descriptions to the battle report. Default: On.

  • Battle Frame LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    Frame the battlefield: red while the battle is undecided, blue once it is won. Off draws no frame. Default: On.

  • Battle Experience LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    List the experience each party member earned in the Battle complete summary. Off: the summary lists only what was collected. Default: On.

  • Maximum Action Popups LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    Choose how many action messages can appear over the map at once. Off hides them; messages still appear in the lower-right text area.

  • Simple Notes Mode LOOK AND FEEL ONLY

    On: the Journal shows only My Notes. Off: every tab. Nothing is deleted. Default: Off.

Wording follows the in-game descriptions. Configure also has Audio and Display sections.
