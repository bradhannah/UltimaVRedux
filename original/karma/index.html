The original game / Karma

Karma

Karma is one number for the whole party, from 0 to 99, and a new game starts at 75. Here is every change the Game Facts record, and what your karma decides.

Range
0–99
Shared by the party. Changes stop at either end.
New game
75
Where every new game starts.
Worst act
−10
Attacking a person who cannot fight back.
Best act
+9
A 900-gold offering at a shrine you have completed.

What lowers it

What you doKarmaGood to know
Attack a person in a settlement−5Guards come for you.
…who is lying in a bed, or held in the stocks or manacles−5 more−10 in all.
Fire a town cannon at someone−5Only when the shot removes them.
Open a chest in a settlement−2Any chest: empty, trapped, even Lord British's.
Pick crops or take food from a table−1Anywhere, even in a dungeon room. You gain 1 food.
Order another drink after the barkeep's warning−1The warning comes after your third drink.
Give Blackthorn the mantra he demands−5That shrine is closed to you.
Give certain answers in conversation−1Each time, as written into that character's dialogue.

A settlement is any town, castle, keep or dwelling: the places on the Atlas that are not dungeons.

What raises it

What you doKarmaGood to know
Free a prisoner from the stocks or manacles+2Jimmy it with a key. A failed try uses up the key. Once per prisoner.
Complete a shrine quest+3Humility gives +6; the other virtues add stats instead.
Make an offering at a completed shrine+1 per 100 goldOffer 100 to 900. At karma 99 you still pay.
Give a beggar money+1Only when the beggar counter is full. Give away your last coin then for +2 more.
Give certain answers in conversation+1Each time, as written into that character's dialogue.

Beggars only reward you now and then. A hidden counter goes up by one at each of the game's regular party updates, as you play and as you rest, and must reach 100. A reward empties it. Giving before then costs gold and earns nothing, and the amount never matters.

What karma decides

  • Resurrection costs experience. When a spell or scroll brings someone back, their XP is multiplied by your karma as a percentage: at karma 60 they keep 60%. Only karma 98 or 99 keeps it all. Their level and maximum HP are then worked out again from the new XP.
  • If the whole party dies, you hear a report chosen by your karma, the dead come back with that same XP cut, and only then is karma raised to at least 75. The party wakes in Castle Britannia at 6 o'clock in the morning.
  • The Wanderer's report is chosen by your karma in bands of 20 (0–19, 20–39, 40–59, 60–79, 80–99). See Levelling and the Wanderer.
  • Some characters say certain things only when your karma is at or above a value written into their dialogue.

Myths about karma

BUSTED KARMA-0005

Lord British doesn't mind you opening his chests.

Every chest opened in a town, castle, keep or dwelling costs 2 karma, whoever owns it, even one that turns out empty or trapped.

BUSTED KARMA-0011 KARMA-0012

The more you give a beggar, the more karma you get.

The reward is +1 whatever the amount, and only once the beggar counter has reached 100. The one bonus is +2 more for giving away your last coin.

IT DEPENDS KARMA-0015

Resurrection brings a character back just as they were.

Only at karma 98 or 99. Below that, their XP is multiplied by your karma as a percentage, and their level and maximum HP can fall with it.

Redux differs

A town cannon's shot stops at walls, so it cannot remove someone behind one. See Differences you'll notice.
