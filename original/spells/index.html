Spells and reagents

All 48 spells: the reagents each one needs, where it can be cast, and what it does in the original.

SPELL-0002 Original: Established MIX-0001 Original: Established SPELL-0001 Original: Incomplete

Mix a spell with the M command, then cast it with C. A spell costs MP equal to its circle. Each ingredient you pick uses one unit per mixture. Order does not matter, but a missing or extra ingredient makes a wrong recipe. Prepared stock tops out at 99.

Where: you can cast it outdoors, in town, in a dungeon or in combat. Being able to cast a spell somewhere does not promise the effect works there. The effects are quoted from the Game Facts table and are technical: D30 and R(a,b) stand for random rolls.
