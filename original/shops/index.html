How prices work

The character who talks to the merchant is the buyer. A smarter buyer pays a smaller markup on the base price. Healer fees, tavern meals, ordinary drinks and wine do not change with Intelligence. Prices round down to whole gold.

Base priceBuyer INT 10Buyer INT 20Buyer INT 30
100170140110

The INT columns in the tables below show the price for buyers of Intelligence 10, 20 and 30. The Game Facts establish no haggling and no karma markup.

Inns

The base price is per person for a night. The inn charges it for everyone in your party, after your Intelligence adjusts it. An inn has room for only so many guests.
