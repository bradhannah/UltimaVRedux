The original does not list a separate cost for each key. An accepted command costs one step of the place you are in; see below. Cancelling inside a command does not always make it free.

Time

Calendar
60 · 24 · 28 · 13
Minutes per hour, hours per day, days per month, months per year.
Outdoors
2 min
Per accepted command, such as a step on grass.
Town
1 min
Per accepted command.
Dungeon
1 min
Normally charged before you choose.
  • Pass (Space) takes a normal step, except an outdoor attack Pass, which is free.
  • Quick save (Q) takes no time outdoors or in towns.
  • In combat, one minute passes before every tenth creature or character acts. It is not every tenth round.
  • Quickness halves each request, to at least 1 minute. Time Stop stops the clock.
  • Camping runs in 5-minute steps. A bed runs in 10-minute steps. Repairing a ship takes about 27 minutes if nothing interrupts it.
  • Sailing with the wind waits 2 minutes at a time, or 1 minute with enhanced rigging.
