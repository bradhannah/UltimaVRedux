Levelling and the Wanderer

You never level up the moment you earn the XP. The Wanderer does it, and he only appears after a qualifying rest outdoors. Players often call him Lord British's ghost; this site uses the name in the Game Facts, the Wanderer. Here is every gate, in the order the game checks them.

The seven steps of a visit

  1. Outdoors only. A camp in a dungeon never qualifies.
  2. 6 hours or more. What counts is the number you chose, not the time you actually slept.
  3. No ambush. An ambush cancels the healing, the visit and the cooldown.
  4. Cooldown has run out. 14 hour changes since your last qualifying rest, counting the hours you just slept.
  5. Healing. Everyone except the guard, the poisoned and the dead gains 1 to 63 HP, up to their maximum. Mages and the Avatar get MP equal to INT; Bards get half their INT.
  6. The visit roll. 1 in 4. On a hit, he appears.
  7. Cooldown resets to 14, whether or not he came.

What he gives

  • Every member who is not dead is restored to full HP and set to Good.
  • Each member's level is recalculated from their XP: 1 plus the number of binary digits in XP ÷ 100 (dropping fractions).
  • If the level differs from the one stored, it is updated and both HP values become 30 × level. One of STR, DEX or INT, picked at random, rises by 1, to at most 30.
  • A report is read out, chosen by your karma in bands of 20. The top band has its own Wanderer text.

The original gives one stat award per visit, even if you gained several levels at once.

Redux differs

You get one stat award for each level gained, and none when a level is corrected downward. See Differences you'll notice.

Myths about the Wanderer

BUSTED CAMP-0005

Wearing the Ankh makes the Wanderer visit more often.

Every qualifying outdoor rest gets the same 1 in 4 chance. The rule lists each condition (outdoors, six hours or more, no ambush, cooldown over) and gear is not among them.

BUSTED CAMP-0005

The Wanderer can only visit once a month.

The game notes the month of his last visit, but never checks it when deciding whether he can come.

BUSTED CAMP-0005

The Wanderer can find you when you camp in a dungeon.

No. A dungeon camp never rolls for him. Only outdoor rests do.
