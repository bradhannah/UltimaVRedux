The original game / Camping, step by step

Camping, step by step

Camping is a loop of five-minute steps. Each pass checks for an ambush at the top of an hour, then lets the guard wander. This is how the original does it.

Each five-minute step

  1. Status and rings. The display updates and Regeneration Rings are checked.
  2. Ambush roll. Only if the hour changed since the last step: a 1 in 64 chance.
  3. On an ambush, the loop ends and combat starts. If you posted a guard, the sleepers are woken first; with no guard, they are not.
  4. Otherwise five minutes pass and the sky refreshes.
  5. The guard wanders. If you posted one, a 1 in 4 chance to take a step in a random compass direction, on screen and onto legal ground.

How many ambush rolls?

The game stops once it reaches the target hour, so the final hour change is never tested. Resting H hours in the original gives H − 1 ambush rolls. A 9-hour rest gets 8 rolls: about an 11.8% chance of at least one ambush.

Redux differs

Redux rests for the full time you chose, so a rest can cross one more hour and face one more roll.

Guards and sleepers

  • Poisoned members are remembered but are not put to sleep.
  • A guard does not change the ambush chance. It changes who is awake when the ambush arrives.
  • No poison tick, meal or starvation check runs inside the loop.

When the rest finishes

A full rest of more than five requested hours, with the cooldown at zero, heals the party and may bring the Wanderer. The details are in Levelling and the Wanderer. An ambush skips all of it.

CONFIRMED CAMP-0003

Longer rests risk more ambushes.

Every hour change except the last rolls 1 in 64. A 9-hour rest gets 8 rolls: about an 11.8% chance of at least one ambush.

BUSTED CAMP-0003 CAMP-0004

Posting a guard makes ambushes rarer.

The chance is 1 in 64 per hour either way. A guard matters once you are attacked: the sleepers are woken. Without one, they are not.
