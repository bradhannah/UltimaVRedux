Camping and rest

Camp for one to nine hours. Rest heals the party when it is long enough, and the longer you stay, the more chances for something to find you.

Length
1–9 hours
Zero or Space cancels setup.
Ambush chance
1 in 64
Per hour change, guarded or not.
Healing
1–63 HP
Each eligible member, up to their maximum. Needs 6+ hours.
Wanderer
1 in 4
Outdoors only, after a qualifying rest.

Where you can camp

Outdoors you need dry land and a party on foot. A ship cannot camp; it is repaired instead. A watch is offered only when more than one member is Good or Poisoned, and the guard you pick has to be Good.

Redux differs

Horses stay mounted and a magic carpet goes into your pack. See Differences you'll notice.

Who ambushes you

When an ambush comes, the creature is chosen from a fixed list of eight entries. A dash means that creature never ambushes there. In a dungeon the choice is made before you start resting.

Who is healed

After a rest of six hours or more, each party member gains between 1 and 63 HP, up to their maximum. The guard, the poisoned and the dead are left out. Mages and the Avatar then have MP equal to their INT; Bards get half their INT.
