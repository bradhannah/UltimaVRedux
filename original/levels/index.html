Levels and experience

Your level follows your experience points (XP) and nothing else. Levels are handed out when the Wanderer visits you after a long rest outdoors.

The level formula has no cap of its own, but every XP gain we have checked stops at 9,999. That makes level 8 the highest you can reach.

XP for a kill

The killing blow earns the creature's maximum HP ÷ 4, rounded down, plus 1. All of it goes to the character who lands the blow; nothing is shared with the party. The Bestiary shows the figure for every creature.

How you actually gain a level

XP alone does nothing until the Wanderer finds you. After a qualifying rest outdoors he may visit, and only then are levels recalculated. See Levelling and the Wanderer for every step and the exact odds.
