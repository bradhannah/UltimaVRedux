Myths, tested

Things players believe, checked against the game's own rules.

BUSTED Wearing the Ankh makes the Wanderer visit more often.

Every qualifying outdoor rest gets the same 1 in 4 chance. The rule lists each condition (outdoors, six hours or more, no ambush, cooldown over) and gear is not among them.

BUSTED The XP from a kill is shared by the party.

All of it goes to whoever lands the killing blow: the creature's maximum HP ÷ 4 + 1.

BUSTED Posting a guard makes ambushes rarer.

The chance is 1 in 64 per hour either way. A guard matters once you are attacked: the sleepers are woken. Without one, they are not.
