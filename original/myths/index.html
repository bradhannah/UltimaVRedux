Myths, tested

Things players believe, checked against the game's own rules. Each verdict rests on a rule the Game Facts mark as Established.

BUSTED CAMP-0005 Levelling and the Wanderer

Wearing the Ankh makes the Wanderer visit more often.

Every qualifying outdoor rest gets the same 1 in 4 chance. The rule lists each condition (outdoors, six hours or more, no ambush, cooldown over) and gear is not among them.

BUSTED PROGRESS-0002 COMBAT-0009 Levels and experience

The XP from a kill is shared by the party.

All of it goes to whoever lands the killing blow: the creature's maximum HP ÷ 4 + 1.

BUSTED CAMP-0003 CAMP-0004 Camping, step by step

Posting a guard makes ambushes rarer.

The chance is 1 in 64 per hour either way. A guard matters once you are attacked: the sleepers are woken. Without one, they are not.

CONFIRMED CAMP-0003 Camping, step by step

Longer rests risk more ambushes.

Every hour change except the last rolls 1 in 64. A 9-hour rest gets 8 rolls: about an 11.8% chance of at least one ambush.

IT DEPENDS PROGRESS-0001 Levels and experience

Level 8 is the highest level.

The level formula has no cap, but every XP gain we have checked stops at 9,999, which is level 8.

BUSTED CAMP-0005 Levelling and the Wanderer

The Wanderer can only visit once a month.

The game notes the month of his last visit, but never checks it when deciding whether he can come.

BUSTED CAMP-0005 Levelling and the Wanderer

The Wanderer can find you when you camp in a dungeon.

No. A dungeon camp never rolls for him. Only outdoor rests do.

BUSTED EQUIP-0013 Combat, by the numbers

Strength makes your sword hit harder.

Damage is a roll from 1 to the weapon's rating, minus the target's armour roll. Strength is not part of it.

BUSTED EQUIP-0013 Combat, by the numbers

The Silver Sword slays the undead.

It counts as magical, so the undead take its full damage instead of half. The damage rules give it no extra power against them.

BUSTED COMBAT-0009 RNG-0001 Combat, by the numbers

The more treasure a monster carries, the safer its chest.

The opposite. The Treasure rating that makes a chest likely also makes a trap likely: a Dragon's chest is trapped 98% of the time.

BUSTED KARMA-0005 Karma

Lord British doesn't mind you opening his chests.

Every chest opened in a town, castle, keep or dwelling costs 2 karma, whoever owns it, even one that turns out empty or trapped.

BUSTED KARMA-0011 KARMA-0012 Karma

The more you give a beggar, the more karma you get.

The reward is +1 whatever the amount, and only once the beggar counter has reached 100. The one bonus is +2 more for giving away your last coin.

IT DEPENDS KARMA-0015 Karma

Resurrection brings a character back just as they were.

Only at karma 98 or 99. Below that, their XP is multiplied by your karma as a percentage, and their level and maximum HP can fall with it.
